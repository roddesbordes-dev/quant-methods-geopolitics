Quantitative Methods for Geopolitics and Geoeconomics

Numbers are weapons in geopolitics: learn to use them, and to see when they are used against you. In six sessions you go from no statistics at all to building your own indicators and indices, testing whether sanctions work, and costing a crisis, always on real, current data.

The six sessions

Each session opens with a real puzzle and a guess, then teaches two methods, each in three steps: a short lesson, an activity on real data in your browser, and feedback that settles the puzzle. Each session ends with a task: an exercise, a claim audit or a self-check quiz. Optional steps show how to do every activity in jamovi.

Free tools and books

  • jamovi (desktop) or jamovi cloud (browser): for the optional analysis steps. Read the first chapter of the user guide before session 1.
  • Datawrapper: publication-quality charts and maps for your notes.
  • Navarro and Foxcroft, Learning Statistics with jamovi.
  • Diez, Çetinkaya-Rundel and Barr, OpenIntro Statistics.
  • Huntington-Klein, The Effect: regression and causal reasoning without heavy maths.
  • Cunningham, Causal Inference: The Mixtape.
  • Wilke, Fundamentals of Data Visualization.
  • Bergstrom and West, Calling Bullshit: case studies on misleading statistics.
  • FT Visual Vocabulary: which chart for which message.
